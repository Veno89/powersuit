Both images were produced with the built-in OpenAI image-generation tool and
saved into this project. They are original visual-development targets, not source
textures or shippable 3D assets.

## Turnaround concept

Use case: stylized-concept. Create an original elite powered exosuit named Aegis
Vanguard for a third-person flight-and-rifle game. Use human-proportioned athletic
assault/recon anatomy, layered ceramic armor over a dark mechanical undersuit,
deep-teal secondary plates, a three-facet cyan optical band, restrained copper
hardware, twin annular dorsal turbines, boot/forearm vectoring ports, believable
joint clearances, and sparse functional emissives. Show the same design in front,
side, rear, and three-quarter views with helmet and turbine insets on a neutral
studio background. Do not copy a franchise design, logo, helmet, panel layout, or
symbol. No text or watermark.

Output: `Concepts/aegis_vanguard_concept_v001.png`.
